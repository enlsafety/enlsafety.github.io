# Home safety banners r84

The existing golf banner and three safety campaigns are preserved. Two new campaign illustrations are used as full-scene backgrounds; Korean headings remain HTML for crisp responsive text.

Assets:
- assets/banners/golf-fire-r84.webp (2172 x 724)
- assets/banners/health-check-2026-r84.webp (2172 x 724)

Generated with the built-in image tool, then encoded as WebP without changing the scene. Original generated PNGs are retained outside the repository. The artwork is illustrative and does not document actual employees or inspections.

Prompt set:
1. Ultra-wide 3:1 Korean golf-course fire-prevention editorial illustration. Autumn green, pin flag, dry rough and pine trees; groundskeeper in navy workwear and safety vest inspecting a red extinguisher beside a parked maintenance cart on the right. Calm preventive scene without fire or smoke. Left half open for HTML copy. Pale blue and green with warm amber accents. No text, logo or watermark.
2. Ultra-wide 3:1 annual health-check editorial illustration. Korean healthcare professional with stethoscope and adult employee in navy work clothes reviewing a clipboard on the right; blank calendar and check symbol. Bright welcoming clinic, soft sky blue and turquoise. Left half open for HTML copy. No procedures, needles, text, numbers, logo or watermark.

Campaign scope:
- Fire copy is a practical prevention reminder, not a statement of a universal statutory duty. Official reference: https://forestfire.nifos.go.kr/sys/kfp/knowFireForestList.do
- Health copy addresses 2026 screening 대상자 and uses the user's requested December 31 deadline. It does not claim every employee has the same screening cycle. Official target lookup: https://www.nhis.or.kr/nhis/index.do
- The dated health campaign is omitted from January 1, 2027 (Asia/Seoul) to avoid an expired notice.

Behavior:
- 5-second auto-advance, 700 ms leftward movement with clone boundaries for continuous wrapping.
- No pause button; automatic motion suspends on hover, keyboard focus, hidden page or reduced-motion preference.
- Manual arrows, dots, keyboard navigation and touch swipes remain available.
- The welcome panel uses a 20% white fill (approximately 80% transparency) with backdrop blur; text is fully opaque.
